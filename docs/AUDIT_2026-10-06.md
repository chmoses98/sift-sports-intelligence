## P3 ideas for the owner (not built)

* A compact game hero on secondary tabs (Matchups, Props, Markets…), keeping the full hero on Overview.
* NFL home on wide desktops: put Props to Watch (or the biggest edges) beside the featured game.
* One "Prices updated …" line per slate instead of one per tile when every game shares a capture.
* Readable family names for Markets-board ladder groups ("Bills win by more than X").
* Ranks for rate stats (yards per carry, catch rate) once a direction and sample rule is agreed.
* On phones, Home could show "On the Slate" before "Biggest Matchup Edges".
