What is wrong with the phone experience today

Captured in a real browser at iPhone 15 Pro size (393 by 852), on the current SIFT 2.0 build with the repository’s own fixtures. Numbers are measured from those captures.
Home
  1. 1A build label and a preview pill sit above the first game. About three quarters of the screen passes before one game row appears.
  2. 2Search, a Sports menu and a bookmark are three bordered buttons. Sport is chosen again in the chip row below.
  3. 3The feature card spends its space on “score unavailable” and three equal outlined chips.
Game, top
  1. 1The hero takes about 40% of the screen before any research.
  2. 2Seven or more section tabs scroll sideways. Three and a half are visible and “Game Scripts” is cut mid-word.
  3. 3Matchup headlines are repeated as sentences, then again as tiles.
  4. 4Tile copy is centered and ragged, with icons that carry no information.
Game, scrolled
  1. 1Two of three matchup cards show a flat team logo where one shows a stadium photo.
  2. 2Player names are set over crowd photographs, so legibility depends on the picture.
  3. 3The tab strip is pinned but still hides half its tabs.
Layout
Containers inside containers. Featured Players is a panel holding two-column tiles holding chips, each with the same blue 1 px border and large radius. Containment stands in for spacing.One grouped surface per section. Hairlines and space carry the structure.
Density
Said three times. The Bills run-game mismatch appears in the Game Read, the Key Matchup Drivers and the Matchup Advantages on one Overview. In the classic build, stat tiles cut their own text (“NE–BUF · researc…”).Each fact once, as a rank duel, with the sentence only where it adds meaning.
Navigation
Hidden destinations. Seven or more game tabs scroll sideways, and sport is picked from a menu and again from chips. The five bottom tabs are sound.Four text tabs that always fit. One sport filter, in the content.
Typography
No phone scale. 134 distinct font-size values across 55 stylesheets. Wide-tracked blue eyebrows (“SIFT 2.0 · COMMAND CENTER”, “THE SLATE”) repeat on every block and compete with the content.Seven sizes. Condensed capitals for names and numerals, Barlow for reading.
Spacing
Every layer re-adds padding. In Featured Players the panel sits 9 px from the screen edge and the player name 32 px in, so the reading column narrows with each nesting level.One 16 px gutter on every screen and 44 px minimum touch targets.
Hierarchy
Everything is outlined in the same blue. What the model says, what the market charges and what could go wrong share one palette, so SIFT’s central distinction has no visual identity. Four “In play” rows look identical.Model is ice, market is gold, risk is coral, on every screen.
