Imagery system

Imagery adds excitement, never facts. A photograph appears only where it is verified for that game; everywhere else the club’s own lighting and crest do the work. The three tiers below are decided by the same resolver rules the production hero already uses.

Tier 1 · Verified venue photo
RaidersatChiefs
Kj1595 · CC BY-SA 4.0

Verified venue photo

A real photograph of that team’s home venue, in that era, with credit. Example shown for a Chiefs home game at Arrowhead.

Shown when
Home team, venue and date all match an approved photo
Never
Neutral sites, other tenants, a different era, or a frame with another team’s branding
Credit
Author and licence on the image, in the info sheet

Club lighting, with a player in the game

No verified venue photo exists, so the card is lit in the two clubs’ colours behind their crests. A portrait of a player in this game may sit behind the score, masked and darkened.

Shown when
No Tier 1 photo. The default for every game
Player photo
Only a player on one of the two rosters for this game
Credit
Required for the photo; the lighting is ours
Tier 3 · SIFT night
SIFT

SIFT night

The neutral backdrop: the app header, the Props and Signals screens, and any game that cannot be tied to a club or a venue. Original art, no photograph.

Shown when
Screens that are not one game, or a game with no identifiable home side
Made of
Two lights, two beams and film grain. No stadium, no people
[[ic x ic--s]]No generic stadiumA stadium that is not the game’s stadium is worse than none.
[[ic x ic--s]]No synthetic compositesAI-generated heroes in the repository are not used as sports content.
[[ic x ic--s]]No unrelated playersA player portrait never appears on a game he is not part of.
[[ic x ic--s]]No imagery as dataA photo never carries a score, a time or a status. Those are text.
