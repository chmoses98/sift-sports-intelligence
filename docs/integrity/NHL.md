## Caveats
- Only 17 days / 113 games; the chronological split is 8 / 5 / 4 days.
- Last-prediction-per-ticker is chosen before requiring a quote; tickers whose last row has no two-sided book are quarantined, not back-filled from earlier rows.
- Pre-start leakage is excluded by construction (observed_at <= start - 60 min), but the entry quote is the archive's own capture, not re-verified here.
- first_goal (48,194 rows) is not evaluated; SOG has no Kalshi market and is out of scope.
