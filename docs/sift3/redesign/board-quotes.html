Read-only assessment · nothing was changed

Quote delivery, measured

Users see clean, automatically updating Kalshi prices: no stale warnings, freshness badges or last-updated labels. Freshness is an engineering responsibility. This page records how prices reach the app today, how reliable that path measured on 2026-10-26, and what to improve. No production pipeline, relay or app code was modified.

1 · Findings

The relay is healthy; the GitHub feed is the slow path

Relay (Vercel)

53 of 53 requests answered 200 in the 14:46Z smoke test. Median 138 ms, max 745 ms. Quote age median 0.7 s, max 14.7 s. Straight to Kalshi, the same runner got 7 rate-limit refusals in its burst; the relay got none.

GitHub feed

Publishes every 211 s in the two intervals sampled (not 180 s), then raw.githubusercontent.com holds each file up to 5 minutes. A new publication reached a reader 1 m 21 s to 2 m 6 s later. Oldest quote seen: 4 m 52 s; worst case about 8.5 minutes.

Loop continuity

The 55-minute loop chain is unbroken across the last 30 runs, with 0 to 1 minute hand-offs. Five runs between 18:56Z and 02:57Z show 80 to 111 minutes, above the 70-minute timeout, so they include waiting. Whether publishing paused is unknown.

4 · Your call

Internal rules to approve

  1. Display rule (never shown to users): lists show a stored quote up to 15 minutes old, or 3 minutes when the event is within 2 hours or live; the exact contract shows a price only after a successful refresh or a quote within 30 seconds (2 minutes if the event is more than 2 hours away).
  2. When no reliable quote exists: a list row shows “—” in the price pill with no copy; the contract sheet shows one plain “Price unavailable right now” state with Retry, and hides the fee and break-even rows because they derive from the price. This is the only visible consequence; it contains no timestamp, age or badge.
  3. Approve items 1 to 3 first; item 4 after a short investigation of Kalshi call counts.
